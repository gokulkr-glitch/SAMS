    Submit an achievement

    Record a student achievement for verification.

    Individual achievements require one active student; team achievements may include several.

        Attach a certificate, a photo of the student receiving or participating in the achievement, or both. Any one valid PDF, JPEG or PNG is sufficient; additional files are optional. Up to 3 files; each must be no larger than 1 MiB and all files together no larger than 3 MiB.
